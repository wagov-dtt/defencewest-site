## Overview

LRY Group Companies Pty. Ltd. was founded in Perth, Western Australia, in 2010 and is headquartered there. Its defence business grew out of medical and emergency-response supply to government: aeromedical patient isolation, CBRN containment and decontamination, and deployable medical, shelter and command infrastructure for military, police and civil-defence users.

Offices followed in Singapore (2017), Malaysia (2021), Indonesia (2022) and the Czech Republic (2023), giving the group a footing in three ASEAN markets and in an EU and NATO member state. Over the same period the portfolio moved further into defence and security work: breath-alcohol and drug screening for police and workforce integrity, concealed-person detection for border and custodial operations, uncrewed aerial and ground systems, and layered counter-UAS.

LRY Group now operates as a distributor, manufacturer's representative and prime integrator. It holds its appointments directly from manufacturers in Australia and Europe and represents them to defence, police, border and emergency agencies in Australia and Southeast Asia. The arrangement works in both directions: a Perth manufacturer's breath and drug-testing instruments are distributed exclusively in Malaysia through LRY Group, while allied European technology is offered into Australia with local representation, regulatory handling and sustainment.

The leadership team combines medical specialists with more than two decades of practice in aviation security, diplomatic protection and government security operations

## Capabilities

LRY Group is appointed directly by the manufacturers it represents. With no master distributor or sub-agent in the chain, technical, export and sustainment questions go straight to the principal's engineers. Around those appointments sit in-country representation in five jurisdictions, tender compliance assessment, end-user certificate and export-permit coordination, and assembly, operator training and sustainment. The channel runs outward too, giving Australian manufacturers representation in Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia.

Capability lines:

Uncrewed and counter-UAS: Czech-engineered UAVs on open, operator-controlled flight software, tracked UGVs, and counter-UAS fusing AESA radar, passive RF and EO/IR with jamming and interceptor-drone effectors.
CBRN and medical: BSL-3 isolation, mass decontamination, gas-tight suits, and the NSN-listed patient transport isolator adopted by the RAAF.
Deployable infrastructure: inflatable field hospitals, command posts and shelters tested from -30 °C to +70 °C; off-road trailers.
Force integrity and search: breath-alcohol and oral-fluid drug screening certified to AS 3547.1 and AS/NZS 4760; a handheld detector that indicates people hidden in vehicles in about 30 seconds, without ionising radiation.

Recent work (2026):

September: briefed the High Commission of the Republic of Singapore in Kuala Lumpur on uncrewed systems for disaster response; joined the Royal Malaysia Police forensic science symposium (NAFSLE).

## Discriminators

Direct appointments: LRY Group is appointed by the manufacturers themselves, with no master distributor between the OEM and the end user. Supplier identities are disclosed under mutual NDA.

Diplomatic and trade channels: LRY Group works directly with embassies and high commissions to bring principals into new markets, including the Czech Embassy in Canberra (Land Forces 2026) and, in Kuala Lumpur, the Singapore High Commission and the Ukrainian Embassy.

Appointments on record: exclusive Malaysian distributor for a Perth manufacturer of breath-alcohol and drug-testing instruments; authorised distributor of the Czech-built MMD01 heartbeat detector.

One counterparty, five jurisdictions: Perth head office with offices in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and the Czech Republic for tendering, registration and support.

Certified lines: NATO Stock Number and TGA approval on the patient isolation unit; screening certified to AS 3547.1 and AS/NZS 4760; BSL-3 rated isolation.

Operator-owned data: UAVs run open flight software under the user's control, with no manufacturer-server login or vendor cloud.

Leadership recognition: commendations from Malaysian Armed Forces Headquarters and Battalion 7 of the Royal Malaysia Police General Operations Force for support during FPDA and ACDFIM defence conferences; White House Communications Agency Certificate of Appreciation for a U.S. Presidential visit to Kuala Lumpur; Life Member, Asian Professional Security Association; member, ASIS International.
