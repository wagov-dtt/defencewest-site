## Overview

Thorny Devil Access and Industries was founded in the Pilbara by Managing Directors Reece Dean and Ben Rees Mogg, whose family histories reflect resilience, community strength and lived experience across Western Australia. Reece describes how his grandmother Fay “worked multiple jobs to put food on the table and clothe the kids” and believed that “no child should go through what she had been through after being stolen from her mother” . Ben’s family story also reflects courage and determination, with his great grandmother Sarah rebuilding her life in Australia after hardship and separation, raising her daughter Ruby through challenging circumstances .

Both founders spent more than a decade in FIFO operations across major industrial sites. Their experience revealed gaps in remote supply chains, workforce support and safety systems. This led to the creation of Thorny Devil Access and Industries, a business built to “offer a new solution to businesses in the area” and to create a workplace that “fosters a deep connection with its customers” while helping employees “forge a sustainable and prosperous future” .

These foundations align strongly with the Defence sector’s focus on sovereign capability, regional industry development and Indigenous participation. Thorny Devil’s values emphasise integrity, innovation, quality and community commitment. The company states that it stands apart through “unwavering commitment to integrity and exceptional customer service” and is “deeply committed to the communities we serve” .

As a Supply Nation Certified business with locations in Karratha, Port Hedland and Canning Vale, Thorny Devil is positioned to support Defence organisations operating across WA. Its workwear, PPE, safety equipment, testing services and automated PPE solutions provide reliable and scalable support to Defence aligned industries. Thorny Devil continues to strengthen its Defence readiness through partnerships, compliance and community driven capability.
## Capabilities

Thorny Devil Access and Industries provides a strong capability set that supports Defence organisations operating across Australia. As an Aboriginal owned and Supply Nation Certified business, we deliver access equipment, safety solutions, uniforms and PPE with a commitment to quality, integrity and community. Our values emphasise “unwavering commitment to integrity and exceptional customer service” and being “deeply committed to the communities we serve” .

Our workwear and PPE capability includes major national brands, in house embroidery, direct to film printing and full uniform customisation. We also operate a Workwear Management System that streamlines ordering, provides pre approved uniform catalogues, controls budgets and offers real time tracking. This supports Defence requirements for compliant, scalable and efficient uniform supply. Thorny Devil supplies site safety equipment, hydration and sun protection, rigging hardware, lifting components and hand free safety tools, ensuring Defence personnel and contractors are equipped for demanding environments.

Our automated PPE vending machines improve readiness, reduce wastage and support secure distribution. These machines are already operating at Karratha and Port Hedland airports, demonstrating performance in high traffic and security sensitive locations. Thorny Devil also provides Drug and Alcohol Testing, Respirator Fit Testing and Ear Fit Testing, supported by certified technicians and compliance with AS NZS 4308 2008. This aligns with Defence level workforce safety standards.

Recent examples include supplying uniforms, PPE and safety equipment to Defence adjacent contractors in the Pilbara, supporting aviation and maritime operations with fast lead times, and delivering testing services to organisations that operate under Defence aligned safety requirements. Thorny Devil continues to expand its capability footprint to support sovereign, reliable and community driven Defence industry needs.
