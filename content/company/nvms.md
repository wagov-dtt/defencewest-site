## Overview

Founded in 1989, NVMS has built a strong reputation for delivering innovative solutions in noise and vibration management. As customer needs evolved, so did we - expanding into asset health, precision test and measurement, digital connectivity, and a full range of support services.

At NVMS, we pride ourselves on our hands-on, collaborative approach. Our dedicated teams work closely with industry partners, tailoring solutions, advice, and technologies to meet unique mission requirements. We bring world-class sensing and measurement technologies to key Defence R&D projects, as well as enhancing mission critical systems with COTS solutions. We are bridging the physical and digital worlds, between sensing and simulation.

Partnering with leading technology providers, our solutions help optimise performance, accelerate development, and ensure reliability across:
Structural integrity
Acoustics and noise management
Durability and lifecycle testing
Electrification and power systems
Pressure Sensors & DAQ's
## Capabilities

With over 37 years supporting the industry, NVMS has developed our offering to the market to ensure our customers have the right solutions to meet these challenges. With Globally recognized partners we are able to provide: 

Physical Testing Expertise:                Sound, vibration, force, torque, strain, and inertia.
Design & Engineering Support:              Commissioning services, application engineering, and integration using COTS solutions.
Data Acquisition & Analysis:               Multi-channel analysers and scalable DAQ systems.
Acoustic Signature Analysis Solutions:     Supporting stealth and survivability.
Shakers & Vibration Testing Systems:       Environmental and durability testing for Defence platforms.
Condition Monitoring & Protection:         Mechanical & Electrical condition monitoring and predictive maintenance systems.
Pressure Sensors and DAQ's:                Full range of Pressure Sensors & Calibration systems.
Technical Support & Training:              Specialist support to ensure successful deployment and knowledge transfer.

Being WA based we are positioned to support the local defence industry. We have provided Acoustics Technology toe the ASCE, LDS Industrial Shakers to GWEO as well as supporting the Navy directly with Condition Monitoring Solutions.
