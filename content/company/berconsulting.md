## Overview

BE&R Consulting is an Australian-owned engineering and advisory company with deep roots in the maritime, shipbuilding, energy and defence sectors. Our leadership team brings decades of experience supporting complex marine and defence-related projects, combining strategic insight with practical project delivery expertise. 
Drawing on extensive experience in maritime engineering, port infrastructure, logistics, vessel sustainment, shipyard selection and asset management, BE&R supports defence clients and industry partners throughout the full project lifecycle. Our services range from strategic advisory and feasibility studies through to engineering assurance, project delivery and operational support.
BE&R understands the importance of capability readiness, sovereign industry participation and effective collaboration across government, Defence, prime contractors and supply chain partners. We bring an independent perspective to complex challenges, helping clients make informed decisions, manage risk and deliver successful outcomes.
Today, BE&R continues to expand its defence capabilities by applying proven expertise from the maritime and energy sectors to support Australia’s evolving defence needs. Our focus remains on delivering practical, technically robust and commercially sound solutions that enhance capability, strengthen industry partnerships and create long-term value for our clients and stakeholder

## Capabilities

Strategic Advisory — Defence policy and program advisory aligned to Australian and allied regulatory frameworks
Engineering — Naval, marine and infrastructure engineering across the full asset lifecycle
Marine Fuels — Bunkering design, operations, low-carbon fuels and fuel logistics
Dry docking & Shipyard Selection — Dry dock engineering management, shipyard assessment and audit
Port Infrastructure & Supply Chain Integration — Port and harbour advisory, facilities planning and supply chain integration
Logistics & Disposal — Vessel disposal, single voyage planning and demolition management
Technology & Innovation — Technology evaluation, innovation integration and HSE-in-design for high-risk environments
Safety & Risk — HAZID and safety assessments for nuclear facilities, ports and naval vessels
