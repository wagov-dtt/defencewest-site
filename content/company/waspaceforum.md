## Overview

Established in 2016, WA Space Forum is a Western Australian industry organisation dedicated to connecting and strengthening the state's space sector.

The Forum brings together industry, government, academia and research organisations to facilitate collaboration, knowledge sharing and strategic partnerships across the space ecosystem.

Recognising the increasing convergence of space and defence capabilities, WA Space Forum supports engagement between the civil space, aerospace and defence sectors. Its activities include industry networking, technical presentations, professional events and opportunities for cross-sector collaboration.

In 2026, WA Space Forum formalised its operations through the establishment of WA Space Forum Pty Ltd and the development of an Advisory Council to support its long-term growth and industry engagement.

The Forum aims to strengthen Western Australia's participation in the national and international space economy, including opportunities arising from defence-related space technologies, research and industry partnerships.

## Capabilities

WA Space Forum provides industry engagement, stakeholder coordination, knowledge sharing and partnership development services that support collaboration across Western Australia's space and defence-related sectors.

Key capabilities include:
• Industry engagement: Connecting businesses, researchers, government representatives and technical specialists to facilitate relationships and identify opportunities for collaboration.
• Events and technical knowledge sharing: Organising industry forums, technical presentations and networking events that showcase emerging technologies, research and commercial developments.
• Cross-sector collaboration: Facilitating engagement between space, aerospace, resources, energy and defence-related industries, recognising opportunities for technology transfer and shared capabilities.
• Stakeholder engagement: Building relationships with industry associations, research institutions and government stakeholders to support sector development.

For example, recent activities include hosting a technical presentation on current rocket launch activities and associated WA university research in 2026, facilitating an industry visit to NEXTDC's Perth data centre infrastructure, and participating in Land Forces 2026 and the ADM WA Defence Conference.

These activities demonstrate the Forum's ability to connect stakeholders, communicate technical developments and support industry engagement relevant to the defence and space sectors.

• Established WA industry network with a focus on connecting the space ecosystem across government, industry, academia and research.
