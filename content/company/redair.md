## Overview

Red Air is the defence arm of Drone Inspections WA Pty Ltd, a Perth company operating uncrewed aircraft commercially under CASA Remotely Piloted Aircraft Operator's Certificate 1174.

The defence work grew from that civil operating base. The company's director previously established and ran a test and training range overseas of roughly 300 square kilometres, which sat outside the host nation's civil aviation system under a safety framework he wrote and for which he was the appointed risk holder. Allied and partner nations flew their own uncrewed aircraft there, weaponised and beyond visual line of sight, including systems still in development. A training academy and an in-country manufacturing capability were established alongside it.

Red Air applies that experience in Western Australia. The company provides threat aircraft and attritable systems for force training, builds and sustains uncrewed aircraft, and carries the airspace, range design and regulatory approvals work that allows others to fly. It is currently in pre-proposal discussion with CASA's Office of Airspace Regulation on segregated airspace for a Western Australian test and training range, and is working with other Western Australian companies on that proposal.

Remote pilot training is delivered through FlyIQ, the group's CASA-approved training school.
## Capabilities

AIRSPACE, RANGE AND APPROVALS
- Range and test-area design, airspace deconfliction and range safety governance
- Safety cases written from a blank sheet to the standard a national aviation authority will accept
- Operations under CASA ReOC 1174, including beyond visual line of sight, extended visual line of sight, night, and above 400 feet
- Currently in pre-proposal discussion with CASA's Office of Airspace Regulation on segregated airspace for a Western Australian range

THREAT REPLICATION AND ATTRITABLE SYSTEMS
- Uncrewed threat aircraft for force training and counter-uncrewed-aircraft activity
- Attritable systems designed so that realistic training can be afforded and expended

BUILD AND SUSTAINMENT
- Design and assembly from component level through to composite airframes of six metres
- Australian-manufactured flight controllers and electronic speed controllers to Red Air designs
- Composites, in-house 3D printing and assembly jigs; fixed, mobile or containerised production cells

TRAINING AND FORCE INTEGRATION
- Operator, maintainer, instructor and mission commander pathways aligned to defence training doctrine
- Workshop-led courses in which students build, configure, diagnose and repair their own airframes
- Combined-arms serials alongside other force elements
- Remote pilot licensing through FlyIQ, a CASA-approved training school
