## Overview

DUG Technology represents the convergence of scientific excellence and sustainable computing innovation. The company’s journey from applied physics specialists to global technology leaders has been marked by continuous innovation in the application of high performance computing (HPC).

DUG enables organisations worldwide to tackle their most complex data challenges through its reliable cloud-based network of HPC facilities, proprietary software solutions, energy-efficient immersion cooling systems and tailored services.

The company’s novel immersion cooling technology is sold globally as part of an exclusive licence agreement and is the cornerstone of DUG’s mobile, edge-computing solution — DUG Nomad.

## Capabilities
DUG delivers proprietary software, cloud-based HPC as-a-service, immersion-cooling, and edge-computing solutions backed by bespoke support for technology onboarding. With decades of experience in algorithm development, optimisation and low-level HPC craft, DUG enables its clients to solve complex problems.

DUG HPC Cloud is a complete environment with expertly-supported, powerful, bare-metal compute and storage allowing clients to scale their workloads. Energy efficiency is realised with DUG Cool, a novel, single-phase immersion-cooling solution with an elegantly simple design that translates to very low maintenance. DUG Nomad combines all of DUG’s know-how to enable high-density HPC and low-latency, high-throughput AI-inference workloads anywhere on Earth — a mobile, scalable, data-centre solution engineered for durability and rapid deployment, with complete sovereign control.
