## Overview

Zetta Pty Ltd was established in Perth, Western Australia in 2004, providing managed IT and cybersecurity services to corporate and government clients across the state for over two decades. Built on a foundation of local ownership, WA-based delivery, and a people-first culture, Zetta has grown into one of Perth's trusted sovereign IT partners — serving clients who require enterprise-grade technology outcomes with on-the-ground accountability.

With the WA defence sector expanding rapidly — driven by AUKUS, the Henderson Defence Precinct, and significant Commonwealth investment in naval sustainment and sovereign capability — Zetta is now actively pursuing opportunities to bring its managed IT and cybersecurity capability to defence primes and government agencies operating in this environment.

Zetta's capabilities are directly aligned to the needs of the defence supply chain. Our managed IT services are built around the ACSC Essential Eight framework at Maturity Level 2, and we hold SMB1001:2026 Level 3 Gold certification through CyberCert — the highest recognised SMB cybersecurity standard in Australia. Our ISO-aligned Quality Management System and ISM-aligned governance frameworks provide the rigour and traceability that defence procurement demands.

We deliver across three core pillars: WA Sovereignty — a 100% local workforce with no offshore escalation; Modern End-User Experience — cloud-first managed IT, Microsoft 365, and Intune device management for distributed defence workforces; and Hybrid Support — flexible on-site and remote IT support integrated into client teams across facilities and contract phases.

Zetta is registering on the WA Defence Industry and Science Capability Directory and looks forward to supporting the growth of WA's sovereign defence industry ecosystem.
