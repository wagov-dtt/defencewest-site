## Overview

Sunfish Robotics is redefining ocean intelligence with a new class of nomadic, persistent subsea underwater vehicles. Backed by combined decades of expertise in subsea engineering, systems integration, and operational deployment, we enable resilient, continuous awareness in contested and remote environments for joint forces and critical infrastructure protection missions.
## Capabilities

Our solar-powered autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) enables months-long operation, surfacing to recharge and transmit data and diving to sit on the seafloor to wait out any storm.
