## Discriminators

As IoT and AI specialists, Forward Logic is proud to be the trusted Australian integration partner of Lumana. Lumana is an enterprise-grade AI video security platform that converts any standard surveillance camera into a continuously learning intelligence agent, reducing false alerts by up to 90%.
In the defence and public sector domains, Lumana provides situational awareness, critical infrastructure protection, and strict regulatory compliance.
•	Situational Awareness: Lumana is used for large-scale event monitoring, crowd management, and threat identification across defence and government sectors.
•	Critical Infrastructure Protection: The platform continuously monitors for perimeter breaches, weapons detection, fire, and violence across secured military, federal, and civilian sites.
