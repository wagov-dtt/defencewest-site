## Overview

DefencIQ is an Australian cybersecurity and Defence compliance consultancy purpose-built for SMEs operating in the Australian Defence supply chain. DISP membership, Essential Eight Maturity Level 2, and ISM-aligned governance are increasingly embedded in Defence contracts and Prime subcontractor requirements. DefencIQ delivers a structured five-phase pathway — Assess, Uplift, Govern, Certify, Operate — through six named services: DISP Readiness Assessment, Essential Eight Uplift, Security Management Framework, DISP Submission & CSQ, vCISO Retainer, and DISPaaS. For organisations wanting full ownership, DISPaaS combines all five services under one engagement with DefencIQ accountable for the outcome — not just the advice. Purpose-built for Defence subcontractors, engineering firms, technology providers, and professional services organisations. Independent, unaffiliated, and member of AIDN.
