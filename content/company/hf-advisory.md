## Overview

HF Advisory was founded in May 2026, a young company in name only. Established by Gary Hale, it formalises work built over four decades at the intersection of defence, national security, space, cyber security, advanced manufacturing and critical minerals, focused on innovation, partnerships and workforce skilling - building national resilience.

Gary's career began with twenty years in the Royal Australian Air Force, rising to Wing Commander across command, capability development, strategic policy and program management roles spanning air traffic control radar, satellite communications, fast jet and space-based infrared programs, underpinned by his Master of Science in Space Operations from the US Air Force Institute of Technology. He then spent twelve years at Cisco Systems, leading global space, media and cyber security innovation across some of the world's most complex markets.

In 2017 he took his first chief executive role, leading cyber security start-up Sapien Cyber through its formative years. From 2018, Gary spent seven years at Curtin University: first as Chief Information Security Officer, giving him first-hand experience as the customer of cyber security services; then as Chief Security Officer and Director of Defence and Space; and finally Director of the National Resilience and Security Program Office, leading skilling, research and innovation, and the DISP and Foreign Arrangements Scheme frameworks that let universities engage safely in Defence.

Across every one of those roles, Gary has built a reputation as someone trusted to do the right thing when bringing parties together. HF Advisory puts that trusted breadth to work: an independent practice building a whole-of-nation approach to national resilience across defence, space, cyber, advanced manufacturing and critical minerals. It carries an active NV-1 clearance, deep relationships across government, industry and academia, and live engagements underway across WA's defence ecosystem.
