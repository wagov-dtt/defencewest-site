## Overview

Magellan Power is a Western Australian designer and manufacturer of high-reliability power and energy systems, established in Perth in 1991. The company specialises in locally engineered AC and DC UPS systems, industrial battery chargers, power conversion systems, battery energy storage and stand-alone power solutions for mission-critical applications. With in-house engineering, R&D, manufacturing and service capabilities, Magellan Power develops robust solutions for demanding operating environments, including Defence and critical infrastructure. Our focus on Australian engineering, local manufacturing, reliability and long-term technical support positions Magellan Power to contribute to Australia's sovereign industrial capability and Defence supply chain.
## Capabilities

Magellan Power provides engineering, design, manufacture, integration, testing, commissioning and lifecycle support for mission-critical power systems. Key capabilities include customised AC and DC UPS systems, industrial battery chargers, DC power systems, power conversion equipment, lithium and industrial battery systems, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), renewable energy integration and stand-alone power solutions.

Our engineering capability enables power systems to be tailored to specific operational requirements and demanding environments. Magellan Power also provides field service, commissioning, maintenance, battery testing, replacement and technical support.

These capabilities are applicable to Defence infrastructure and applications requiring reliable and resilient power, including naval, land and air applications, communications, control systems, facilities, remote installations and other critical electrical infrastructure.
