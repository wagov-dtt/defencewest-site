## Overview

Stantec empowers clients, people and communities to rise to the world’s greatest challenges. 
We are a global leader in sustainable engineering and environmental consulting. Our professionals deliver the expertise, technology and innovation communities need to manage aging infrastructure, demographic and population changes, the energy transition, and more. 
Today’s communities transcend geographic borders. At Stantec, community means everyone with an interest in the work that we do—from our project teams and industry colleagues to our clients and the people our work impacts.  
The diverse perspectives of our partners and interested parties drive us to think beyond what’s previously been done on critical issues such as climate change, digital transformation, and future-proofing our cities and infrastructure. 
We are designers, engineers, scientists, project managers and strategic advisors. Together, we advance communities everywhere by redefining what’s possible through innovation, creativity and client relationships.

## Capabilities

ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES – TERRESTRIAL
 Stakeholder consultation; terrestrial, aquatic and subterranean ecology; environmental impact assessment, approvals, planning and management; flood, hydrologic, hydraulic, hydrogeological and aquifer modelling; rehabilitation, restoration and mine closure; natural capital valuation; vegetation assessment; social impact; soil science and geochemistry; stormwater management; and UAV surveying.

ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES – MARINE
 Ecological assessment and permitting; marine habitat, wildlife and biodiversity surveys; water and sediment quality assessment; benthic and contaminant characterisation; coastal, hydraulic and hydrologic modelling; intake/outfall assessments; thermal, effluent, sediment and plume modelling; oceanography and coastal geomorphology; ecotoxicity and ecological risk; spill response; habitat restoration; rare species surveys; and natural capital/damage assessment.

PLANNING, FEASIBILITY, ENGINEERING & DESIGN
 Dams, reservoirs, CSG ponds, pipelines, pump stations, terminals, refineries, processing facilities, water/wastewater infrastructure, design verification and EPCM.

PROJECT DELIVERY
 Construction, project and program management; project controls.

BUSINESS & MANAGEMENT CONSULTING
 Field auditing; asset management; funding strategies; quality, safety and risk management; contract administration and verification; BIM.

BUILDINGS ENGINEERING
 Acoustics, AV, civil, structural, electrical, mechanical, hydraulics, fire protection and fire safety, energy, sustainability, security, smart buildings, lighting, ICT/telecoms, property asset management and vertical transportation.
