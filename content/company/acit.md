## Overview

ACIT Global’s connection to the defence industry comes from its Director, Travis Hoult, a Navy veteran who served 11 years in uniform as a Communications and Information Systems sailor. Since leaving the Navy, he has built an extensive career supporting defence industry organisations. That experience shapes how ACIT Global works. We understand the security discipline, operational pressure and compliance expectations that defence customers and their supply chains face. We bring that background to managed IT, cybersecurity and Microsoft 365 services for Western Australian organisations.
## Capabilities

ACIT Global delivers IT capabilities that suit defence industry organisations, where security and compliance come first.

Essential Eight and Microsoft 365 security: We assess and harden Microsoft 365 tenants against the ASD Essential Eight, covering identity, conditional access, email security and data protection, with gap registers and remediation plans.

Network security: We design, audit and harden firewall and network environments, including WatchGuard platforms, to reduce attack surface and meet customer security requirements.

Managed IT services: We provide proactive support, patching, monitoring and lifecycle management for secure, reliable operations.

Secure cloud and AI readiness: We prepare environments for the safe adoption of tools such as Microsoft Copilot, with data governance in place first.

Our Director’s 11 years of naval communications and information systems service means we understand how these capabilities apply in defence environments.
