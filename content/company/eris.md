## Overview

Eris is an Australian, veteran-owned owned and operated workforce partner delivering 20 years of experience to complex projects in high compliance environments.

By providing end-to-end workforce solutions, we take away the administrative burden from our clients.

Project resourcing, onboarding, personnel logistics, industrial relations, payroll, and training is coordinated by our experienced and capable team.
## Capabilities

RESOURCING & RECRUITMENT
Direct sourcing, screening, and placement of trusted crews who are work ready and aligned to project needs.

PERSONNEL LOGISTICS
End-to-end planning and management of travel, accommodation, transfers, mobilisation, and rosters.

TRAINING & COMPETENCY
We manage all training bookings and track competencies and expiries so crews remain compliant for the entire life of your project.

PAYROLL & AWARD MANAGEMENT
We're specialists in interpreting multiple EBAs and complex pay rules, delivering accurate payments and transparent reporting across projects.

INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS MANAGEMENT
Risk-managed IR support across engagement, EBA compliance, and stakeholder relationships to stabilise workforce environments.

CLIENT REPRESENTATION & WORKSITE INSPECTIONS
On site representation, workplace suitability checks, and audit inspections.

ROPE ACCESS SERVICES
Probationary IRATA Member (Operator) providing qualified Rope Access Personnel and complying with the regulatory requirements and applicable policies of IRATA International.

MARINE CREW & CATERING SERVICES
Provided via our marine joint venture partner, Australian Offshore Solutions.

FABRICATION SERVICES
Supported via our joint venture partner, Steel Diamond, for the complex fabrication of carbon and exotic steel.
