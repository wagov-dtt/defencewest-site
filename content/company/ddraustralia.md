## Overview

DDR has established itself as a trusted partner to the Australian Defence sector, delivering construction, maintenance, refurbishment and infrastructure upgrade projects across Defence establishments nationwide. Through our dedicated Defence division, which operates exclusively within Defence environments, DDR has developed tailored systems, processes and governance frameworks that align with the Department of Defence’s strategic objectives, operational requirements and compliance obligations.
Our extensive experience working across Defence bases throughout Australia has provided a strong understanding of live operational environments, stakeholder engagement, security requirements and the challenges associated with maintaining and upgrading critical infrastructure. Supported by a Defence-focused workforce and proven delivery methodology, DDR consistently delivers safe, efficient and high-quality outcomes with minimal disruption to operations.
This specialised focus enables DDR to support the ongoing capability, functionality and resilience of the Defence Estate while providing reliable, value-driven solutions that meet Defence’s evolving needs.
## Capabilities

DDR provides specialist construction, maintenance, refurbishment and infrastructure upgrade services tailored to Defence environments. Our core capabilities include delivering works within live operational facilities, hazardous materials management, HVAC and mechanical upgrades, electrical, security and communications infrastructure, building refurbishments, compliance upgrades and asset sustainment works.
A key differentiator is our dedicated Defence division, which operates exclusively within Defence environments and has established systems and processes aligned with Defence requirements, security protocols and operational priorities. Our team is experienced in stakeholder management, contractor coordination, risk management and maintaining operational continuity throughout project delivery.
Recent Defence projects have included hazardous materials remediation, building refurbishment works, HVAC replacements and upgrades, electrical, security and communications infrastructure improvements, and maintenance activities across Defence establishments. These projects were delivered safely and efficiently while minimising disruption to base operations and maintaining compliance with Defence, safety and environmental requirements.
Supported by robust project management systems, experienced personnel and a trusted subcontractor network, DDR consistently delivers reliable, value-driven outcomes that enhance the functionality, safety and resilience of the Defence Estate.
