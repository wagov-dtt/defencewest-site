## Overview

AusWest Defence Security Pty Ltd is a Western Australian, veteran owned advisory business established in 2026 to help small and medium enterprises enter and grow within the Defence sector.

The business was founded by an Australian Army veteran and former aircraft technician with more than 25 years' experience across defence, veterans affairs, not for profit and aviation. That experience shapes a practical approach. Many capable WA businesses have exactly what Defence needs but find the security and compliance requirements difficult to navigate.

AusWest sits beside its clients and guides them through those requirements one step at a time, from first questions through to DISP membership and ongoing compliance, so they can work with Defence and the primes with confidence.
## Capabilities

• DISP and security compliance: gap assessments, advice on the right membership level, security policies and plans, application lodgement and liaison through to outcome, and ongoing compliance support.
• Personnel security and clearances: personnel screening procedures aligned to AS 4811:2022, security clearance planning for key staff, guidance on working with AGSVA, and security awareness sessions for teams.
• Defence industry advisory: governance, contracts and tenders, industry connections, and communications and social media that help businesses present credibly to Defence and the primes.
• Strategic security support: support for in house Security Officers, physical security and zone planning guidance, classified information handling procedures, and coordination of trusted cyber and ICT partners.
