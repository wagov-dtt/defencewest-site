## Overview

Kerman, through our long term employees, an in-depth history working for Defence, from design, construction, commissioning and administration of projects. These have predomintaely been in Western Australia (Garden Island, Campbell Barracks, Pearce airbase and north west bases. 
## Capabilities

Through Kerman's diverse design & construction experience, we can deliver solutions to Defence to their critical infrastructure needs in a cost effective way, including camps, fuel facilities, hangars, warehouses, workshops, water treatment process, EO Facilities. Kerman has been engaged by Austal to look at design & constructing their shipbuilding facilities. 
## Discriminators

Kerman Contracting is a Western Australian-operated company. 
We hold:
>OFSC accreditation 
>ISO9001 certification
>ISO14001 certification
>ISO45001 certification 
The company has extensive experience in the design and construction of fuel and hydrocarbon facilities, bulk materials storage and handling infrastructure, large-scale workshops and warehouse facilities, and accommodation facilities —across both metropolitan and remote locations in WA and interstate.
With over 45 years of operation, Kerman has built a strong reputation for its project delivery supported by robust internal management systems and transparent reporting—both of which, aligning with the client/end-user expectations and successful delivery of Defence projects. These systems are designed to support structured governance, compliance with stringent regulatory frameworks, and clear communication across all project phases. This capability and approach has led to a high rate of repeat business and numerous sole-sourced or negotiated contracts.
Kerman has a number of personnel in the business with previous Defence experience and security clearances. Kerman also has a pending application for DISP accreditation.
