## Overview

Established in Perth in 1993, Adarsh Australia is an Australian-owned, family-operated manufacturing and engineering company with over 30 years of experience in precision manufacturing.

Over the years, Adarsh has expanded its capabilities and involvement in the Australian defence industry, working with defence organisations and sub-contractors.

Originally specialising in precision CNC machining, Adarsh has grown into a multidisciplinary manufacturer offering 5-axis machining, CNC turning and milling, wire EDM, metal fabrication, mechanical engineering, reverse engineering, industrial 3D printing and plastic part manufacturing.

With manufacturing facilities in Malaga and Kwinana Beach, Western Australia, Adarsh supports the defence supply chain through the manufacture of precision components, prototypes, tooling and fabricated assemblies.

The company continues to invest in advanced manufacturing equipment, facilities and skilled personnel to strengthen its local capabilities and support Australia's defence industry.
## Capabilities

Adarsh Australia provides a broad range of manufacturing and engineering services to the Australian defence industry, including precision CNC machining (milling, turning and 5-axis machining), wire EDM, metal fabrication, casting and forging, industrial 3D printing and plastic part manufacturing.

Our manufacturing facilities in Malaga and Kwinana Beach are equipped to produce complex metal and plastic components, fabricated assemblies, prototypes and production parts. Our in-house engineering team also provides CAD design, reverse engineering, 3D scanning and design for manufacture (DFM).

Adarsh has experience supplying Australian defence contractors, including ASC, Raytheon, Austal, BAE Systems, Rheinmetall and the Department of Defence.

Recent defence related work has included the manufacture of precision-machined metal and plastic components for Australian defence customers. Our capabilities support the production of custom components, specialist tooling and replacement parts, from one-off prototypes through to repeat manufacturing.

As an example, we recently reverse engineered and machined mechanical seals for old machinery on a defence site in WA. We have also machined custom components for subsea, aerosopace, and demanding land environments, among a wide range of other projects.

By combining engineering, machining, fabrication and other manufacturing processes, Adarsh can manage multiple stages of production under one company, reducing reliance on separate suppliers.

We continue to expand our local manufacturing capabilities through investment in advanced equipment, facilities and skilled personnel, supporting Australia's defence industry and strengthening domestic supply chains.
