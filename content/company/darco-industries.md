## Overview

Darco Industries is an import/wholesale business established in July 2010 by James Dar BSc who has technical sales experience since 1980. Since 1998 his time has specifically been with high temperature textiles and advanced industrial insulation materials.
Based in Perth, Western Australia, our customer focused staff has a proven record of being very responsive to customer needs and special testing demands. Our client base is worldwide and consists of the automotive OEM’s, heavy-duty engine OEM’s, industrial suppliers and hydraulic companies.

## Capabilities

Darco Industries provides high-performance thermal, acoustic, and passive fire protection solutions tailored for defence applications. Key capabilities include supplying high-temperature glass/ceramic fabrics (e.g., 3M Nextel), insulation blankets, custom gaskets, and fire-retardant velcro. Products designed to meet rigorous Military Specifications (Mil-Spec) for maritime, aerospace, and land-based equipment, ensuring safety and fire resistance.
Australia wide we currently work with oem's and shipbuilders such as Austal ships, Rheinmetall & Airbus Australia in supplying materials that are used for manufacturing and maintenance projects, within the defence industry.
