## Overview

Streamline Connect is actively building its defence industry presence by leveraging its proven experience in remote, regional and operational communications infrastructure environments.

While Streamline Connect is not a traditional defence prime, our capability is highly relevant to Defence, defence primes and major contractors requiring practical delivery support for fibre, communications infrastructure, civil pathways, modular technical rooms and site connectivity.

The business has delivered telecommunications, optical fibre, civil works and connectivity solutions across mining, infrastructure, commercial, carrier and regional environments where reliability, safety, coordination and field execution are critical. These same capabilities are directly applicable to defence bases, remote training areas, deployable facilities, maritime infrastructure, sustainment facilities and secure operational sites.

Streamline Connect is now engaging more formally with the defence sector through supplier registrations, industry events, Defence West and prime contractor pathways. Our focus is on supporting Defence and its supply chain with practical, field-ready communications infrastructure that can be scoped, built, installed, commissioned and supported in demanding environments.

Our defence-relevant offering includes turnkey fibre delivery, pits and conduits, trenching, hauling, cable pathways, communications rooms, equipment shelters, modular technical rooms, temporary-to-permanent site connectivity and ongoing maintenance support.

## Capabilities

Streamline Connect provides turnkey fibre, telecommunications, communications infrastructure, civil pathways, modular technical infrastructure and site connectivity solutions that are highly relevant to the defence industry.

Our capabilities include optical fibre design, installation, testing and commissioning; pits, conduits, trenching, hauling and cable pathways; site-wide connectivity infrastructure; communications rooms; equipment shelters; modular technical rooms; carrier coordination; temporary-to-permanent connectivity; and ongoing maintenance and rectification support.

These capabilities can benefit Defence, defence primes and major contractors by supporting reliable communications infrastructure across bases, remote training areas, deployable facilities, maritime infrastructure, workshops, camps, sustainment facilities and secure operational sites.

While Streamline Connect is actively building its direct defence industry profile, our recent experience includes delivery of fibre, telecommunications, civil pathway and site connectivity works across remote, regional, mining, infrastructure and carrier environments. These projects require many of the same delivery disciplines expected in defence environments, including safety, planning, field execution, stakeholder coordination, documentation, testing and commissioning.

Streamline Connect has also delivered regional connectivity works supporting major telecommunications providers and operational sites where reliable communications infrastructure is critical. This experience is directly transferable to defence projects requiring practical, field-ready support for fibre, communications pathways, modular technical rooms and resilient site connectivity.

We are now engaging with Defence West, defence primes and relevant industry pathways to offer these capabilities into the defence supply chain.

* Modular technical infrastructure: Streamline Connect can support communications rooms, equipment shelters, modular technical rooms and deployable connectivity infrastructure suited to remote or operational sites.
