## Overview

Founded in 2016, Geodetica is an Australian-owned professional services company with a strong track record of supporting the Australian Defence Force, the Department of Defence, and the broader defence industry. Since its establishment, the company has delivered specialist expertise across complex acquisition, sustainment, infrastructure and capability programs, supporting clients throughout the Defence Capability Life Cycle. 

Geodetica has successfully supported major Defence programs by providing program and project management, engineering management, commercial and procurement support, governance, risk and assurance, integrated logistics support, and project controls. Our personnel have worked across some of Australia's most significant maritime, land and joint capability projects, partnering with Commonwealth and industry teams to deliver outcomes in complex and highly regulated environments. 

As an Australian small business, Geodetica combines the agility and responsiveness of an SME with the capability and experience expected by Defence. Our approach is founded on collaboration, practical problem solving and trusted client relationships, enabling us to rapidly integrate with client teams while maintaining a strong focus on governance, quality and delivery.

Geodetica is a member of the Defence Industry Security Program (DISP) and is accredited to sponsor Australian Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA) security clearances, allowing us to provide appropriately cleared personnel to support sensitive Defence programs. 

Today, Geodetica continues to expand its presence across Australia, supporting Defence, government and industry clients with experienced professionals who help plan, manage and deliver complex capability outcomes safely, efficiently and successfully.
## Capabilities

Geodetica provides specialist professional services that support the successful planning, acquisition, delivery and sustainment of Defence capability. Our core capabilities include program and project management, engineering management, commercial and procurement management, governance, risk and assurance, integrated logistics support, project controls, scheduling, earned value management and strategic advisory services. We embed experienced personnel within Commonwealth and industry teams to strengthen delivery capability, improve governance and manage complex stakeholder environments. 

Recent examples include supporting the Commonwealth on major maritime acquisition programs through the provision of project management, governance, commercial support and engineering management services. Geodetica personnel have developed acquisition strategies, established program governance frameworks, coordinated complex stakeholder engagement, managed project risks and supported First and Second Pass capability development activities.

Geodetica has also provided specialist scheduling, project controls and earned value management support on Australia's largest Defence acquisition programs, including the development and maintenance of integrated master schedules, integrated baseline reviews, schedule risk analysis and executive reporting to enable informed decision-making and effective program oversight. 

In addition, our personnel currently support Defence through commercial and procurement management, integrated logistics support, engineering coordination, quality assurance, and governance activities across complex maritime capability and sustainment programs. By combining deep Defence experience with practical, outcomes-focused delivery, Geodetica helps clients reduce risk, strengthen governance and successfully deliver capability throughout the Defence Capability Life Cycle.
