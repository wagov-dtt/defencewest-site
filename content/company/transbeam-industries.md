## Overview

TRANSBEAM INDUSTRIES and Bruce Rock Engineering operate within the BRE Group, a family‑owned Australian manufacturing and engineering organisation with heritage dating back to 1980. Established in Western Australia, the Group has progressively evolved from regional manufacturing into a nationally recognised provider of heavy‑duty fabricated solutions supporting Defence‑aligned and regulated industries.

Bruce Rock Engineering has a long history of designing, manufacturing and sustaining complex transport and fabricated systems, underpinned by disciplined engineering practices, quality assurance frameworks and a strong safety culture. To support high‑precision, repeatable and scalable fabrication requirements, Transbeam Industries was established as the Group’s dedicated CNC metal processing and fabrication capability.
With facilities located in Perth (Forrestfield), Bruce Rock, Geraldton and Port Hedland, and a workforce of approximately 500 employees, the Group provides resilient and scalable sovereign manufacturing capacity across Western Australia. Operating from a modern 16,000 sqm Forrestfield facility, Transbeam Industries delivers advanced metal processing services including CNC plate and tubular cutting, pressing and rolling, robotic welding, blasting and painting.

Through sustained engagement with Defence contractors and prime supply chains, the BRE Group has developed a strong understanding of Defence expectations relating to governance, safety, quality management, traceability, schedule discipline and Australian Industry Capability. TRANSBEAM INDUSTRIES and Bruce Rock Engineering continue to support Defence programs by providing reliable, compliant and locally delivered fabrication solutions that contribute to Defence readiness, sustainment and long‑term capability outcomes.

## Capabilities

TRANSBEAM INDUSTRIES and Bruce Rock Engineering provide end‑to‑end metal processing, fabrication and lifecycle support capabilities that directly support Defence contractors and prime integrators. Core capabilities include CNC plate processing, pressing and rolling, tube and section processing, robotic welding, blasting and industrial painting, supported by disciplined quality and safety systems.

These capabilities enable the delivery of repeatable, compliant and traceable fabricated components suitable for Defence infrastructure, land and maritime support equipment, logistics assets and sustainment programs. The Group’s in‑house manufacturing capacity supports both low‑volume, high‑complexity fabrication and higher‑throughput production environments, providing flexibility to meet varying Defence program requirements.
Recent Defence‑aligned activities include supporting Defence contractors with fabricated steel components and assemblies for infrastructure and sustainment‑related scopes, leveraging CNC precision, robotic welding and controlled surface treatment to meet demanding quality and durability requirements. Operating across multiple sites in Western Australia enables responsive delivery, surge capacity and reduced supply‑chain risk for Defence programs.

Through continuous investment in advanced manufacturing technology, skilled workforce development and facility expansion, TRANSBEAM INDUSTRIES and Bruce Rock Engineering provide Defence‑ready fabrication capability aligned with CASG and prime contractor expectations for safety, compliance, delivery assurance and Australian Industry Capability outcomes.
