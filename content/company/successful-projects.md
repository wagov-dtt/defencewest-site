## Overview

Successful Projects (SP) is an Australian-owned professional services consultancy that has supported Defence, government and complex infrastructure projects since 2011. Headquartered in Western Australia and operating nationally, SP delivers project planning advisory, program and project controls, contract administration, project management and workforce capability development through specialist training and advisory services. 

Over more than a decade, SP has built its Defence capability by supporting organisations that require strong governance, schedule certainty, commercial discipline and delivery assurance across complex project environments. The company combines practical project delivery experience with advanced technical expertise in planning, controls and contract administration to help clients improve performance and make informed project decisions.

Today, SP comprises a 30-strong core team and continues to invest in sovereign capability through workforce development, mentoring and industry engagement. 

The company is a DISP Member, JOSCAR registered and maintains personnel holding individual security clearances. Supported by ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certified management systems, SP provides trusted services to Defence and government clients requiring secure, reliable and outcome-focused project support. 

Through its commitment to quality, continuous improvement and professional excellence, SP helps clients deliver complex programs with confidence while strengthening Australia's Defence industry capability
## Capabilities

Successful Projects provides specialist capability in project planning advisory, project controls, project management, contract administration and Primavera P6 training. These services support Defence organisations to improve schedule certainty, delivery assurance, governance, commercial performance and workforce capability across complex programs. Supported by ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certified management systems, DISP membership, JOSCAR registration and personnel holding individual security clearances, SP is well positioned to support Defence and national security-related projects. 

Our recent experience demonstrates the practical value of these capabilities. SP provided planning, controls and delivery support on a Defence-related capital project valued at approximately $355 million, contributing to the project being completed 6 months ahead of schedule. We have also supported State Government programs through the implementation of robust program controls, governance and contract administration frameworks that reduced non-entitled claims by approximately 50%, improving transparency and protecting project outcomes. In addition, SP is currently supporting a major Defence program through project controls, planning and delivery assurance services designed to maintain schedule performance and support on-time project completion.

A key differentiator for SP is the combination of experienced practitioners, advanced technical capability and a strong focus on knowledge transfer. Our team not only delivers project outcomes but also builds client capability through mentoring, training and the implementation of sustainable systems and processes. This approach helps Defence organisations improve decision-making, strengthen governance and enhance delivery performance across the full project lifecycle
