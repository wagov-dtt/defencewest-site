## Overview

Executive Risk Solutions (ERS) is an Australian, veteran-owned and led company with strong foundations in the defence, intelligence, law enforcement and emergency services sectors.

Established by professionals with extensive operational backgrounds, including experience within Australia's Special Air Service Regiment (SASR), ERS was built on the principle of applying specialist operational expertise to protect people, assets and organisations operating in complex and high-risk environments.

Our connection to the Defence industry is embedded in our leadership, workforce and operational approach. ERS has supported Defence through specialist training and capability development, including its engagement with the Royal Australian Air Force Security and Fire School.

Over time, ERS has expanded its national presence and capabilities to support Defence industry partners, government agencies, critical infrastructure operators and organisations with complex security and operational requirements.

ERS is a member of the Defence Industry Security Program (DISP), reflecting our commitment to operating within Australia's Defence security environment. Our partnership with Yampurru, an Aboriginal-owned and operated company, further strengthens our sovereign capability and supports Indigenous participation in Defence supply chains.

Today, ERS combines its Defence heritage, Australian ownership and operational experience to support Australia's Defence industry and its evolving security and resilience requirements.
## Capabilities

ERS delivers integrated security, intelligence, crisis and emergency management and operational support to Defence, primes and their supply chains, helping protect personnel, sensitive assets and operational continuity.

Our core capabilities include:

-Security Advisory and Assurance: Threat and risk assessments, Defence precinct and facility reviews, protective security planning, governance and supply chain due diligence.

-Intelligence and Workforce Security: Protective intelligence, behavioural and pattern-of-life analysis, insider risk programs and intelligence reporting.

-Crisis and Emergency Management: Preparedness, emergency response planning, business continuity, exercises and specialist training.

-24/7 Monitoring and Response: Remote site overwatch, technology-enabled surveillance, incident escalation and response coordination through our Operations Centre.

-Specialist Personnel: Security and emergency management professionals supporting complex, remote and high-risk operating environments.

Relevant Delivery Experience

ERS delivered a specialist instructor development program for the Royal Australian Air Force Security and Fire School, supporting more than 200 instructors and strengthening instructional capability and training performance.

Across critical infrastructure, ERS has delivered security and emergency management services to major Australian ports, including Pilbara Ports and Mid West Ports, and supported aviation and resources operators with emergency preparedness and operational risk management.

These engagements demonstrate capabilities relevant to Defence estates, geographically dispersed assets and critical supply chains, where security assurance, operational resilience and rapid incident response are essential.

ERS combines specialist expertise, onshore intelligence analysis, technology and 24/7 operational coordination to deliver scalable risk mitigation solutions.
