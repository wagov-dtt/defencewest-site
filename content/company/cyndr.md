## Overview

Cyndr is a Western Australian technology company established to address the difficulty of identifying and assessing emerging capabilities across the defence and national security ecosystem. The company developed its capability intelligence platform to bring together fragmented information about technologies, organisations and research, helping users understand what capability exists, where it fits and what evidence supports it. Through engagement with government, defence, industry and research organisations, Cyndr has focused on supporting technology discovery, capability evaluation and ongoing monitoring. Its work helps connect Australian innovation with defence priorities, enabling more informed decisions about capability development, partnerships and sovereign industry opportunities.
## Capabilities

Cyndr provides capability intelligence to support defence planning, technology discovery and industry engagement. Its platform brings together information on organisations, technologies, systems and research, helping users understand available capabilities, assess their relevance to defence priorities and identify potential suppliers and partners.

Core capabilities include mapping technology and industrial ecosystems, evaluating capability options and monitoring emerging developments. By connecting fragmented information with supporting evidence, Cyndr enables more informed decisions across capability development, research investment and sovereign industry development.

Cyndr’s work with UNSW through Defence Trailblazer applies advanced search and evidence retrieval to defence and national security questions. Alongside this research, the company structures government funding, research and industry information to improve visibility of emerging technologies and the organisations developing them.

- Purpose-built for capability intelligence in defence and national security, connecting technology and industry information with capability needs.
