## Overview

Prochem has supplied the Australian Navy and their contractors product since the inception of Hobart, ANZAC, Cape, OPV and Hunter classes. We have product partnerships with Tier1 suppliers to the US Navy including nuclear along with US Coast Guard.
## Capabilities

We have supplied non-welded high pressure fittings initially designed and developed for F4 fighter jets further evolved and now used within Navy and Coast guard applications. Customised specific sized and itemised CUNI pipe and fittings. Specialised valving and filter ranges for maritime both on and below the ocean.
## Discriminators
