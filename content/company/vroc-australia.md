## Overview

VROC is new to the defence sector in 2026 and is currently undertaking robust go-to-market efforts to drive awareness and position our solutions in this sector.  That said, VROC was founded in 2016 in Perth and was purpose-built for asset owners and service providers across Oil & Gas, Mining, the Built Environment and Maritime, VROC enables secure data integration, visualization, automation and predictive AI at scale. ​VROC’s technology extends directly to Defence sustainment and base operations—supporting fleet-wide predictive maintenance, performance optimisation and operational resilience in complex and contested environments, with minimal data science overhead and strong sovereign control.​
## Capabilities

Our services provide industrial and defence clients the ability to: 
a) democratize the use of data within an organization, and utilise a modern approach to data storage, analytics and data visualization and be able to reliably access data and extract real-time valuable insights. 
b) remotely monitor, control and automate an unlimited number of assets and systems, from one centralised IoT platform. 
c) embed automated end-to-end AI enabling engineers, asset operators and SMEs to build AI models without any programming knowledge or experience. 
Our AI offers a unique holistic approach to analysing timeseries data across the entire plant/asset, rather than just individual pieces of equipment, and the platform can detect minute anomalies, with accurate predictions often days-to-weeks in advance. Customer teams can act on these insights, plan interventions, implement predictive maintenance and optimize operations. 
Key Capabilities - Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology Services, Sensor Systems, Predictive Maintenance, Process Optimisation,
Process Control and Automation 
VROC’s capability stack is built on three integrated pillars: 
1. DataHUB+: A secure historian and integration layer (Single Source of Truth). 
2. OPUS: A no-code AutoML platform for predictive analytics. 
3. OASIS: A Web-SCADA/IIoT control and monitoring platform. 
All the above are underpinned by engineering services for hardware/software integration. The stack addresses the ""toughest AI adoption challenges"": real-time OT integration, rapid deployment at scale, and whole-of-life cost effectiveness.
