## Overview

VROC was founded in 2016 in Perth and was purpose-built for asset owners and service providers across Oil & Gas, Mining, the Built Environment and Maritime. VROC's solutions enable secure data integration, visualization, automation and predictive AI at scale. ​VROC’s technology extends directly to Defence sustainment and base operations—supporting fleet-wide predictive maintenance, asset sustainment, performance optimisation and operational resilience across supply chains in complex and contested environments, with minimal data science overhead and strong sovereign control.​
## Capabilities

Our services provide industrial and defence clients the ability to: a) democratize the use of data within an organization, and utilise a modern approach to data storage, analytics and data visualization and be able to reliably access data and extract real-time valuable insights. b) remotely monitor, control and automate an unlimited number of assets and systems, from one centralised IoT platform. c) embed automated end-to-end AI enabling engineers, asset operators and SMEs to build AI models without any programming knowledge or experience. Our AI offers a unique holistic approach to analysing timeseries data across the entire plant/asset, rather than just individual pieces of equipment, and the platform can detect minute anomalies, with accurate predictions often days-to-weeks in advance. Customer teams can act on these insights, plan interventions, implement predictive maintenance and optimize operations. Key Capabilities - Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology Services, Sensor Systems, Predictive Maintenance, Process Optimisation, Process Control and Automation VROC’s capability stack is built on three integrated pillars:

DataHUB+: A secure historian and integration layer (Single Source of Truth).
OPUS: A no-code AutoML platform for predictive analytics.
OASIS: A Web-SCADA/IIoT control and monitoring platform. All the above are underpinned by engineering services for hardware/software integration. The stack addresses the ““toughest AI adoption challenges””: real-time OT integration, rapid deployment at scale, and whole-of-life cost effectiveness.
## Discriminators

 - Battle-tested in live operations - our solutions are already deployed in real industrial environments (O&G, Mining, Utilities, Built Environment) and proven in critical infrastructure and asset-intensive environments.
 - Our solution is built on sovereign and secure architecture - there is no dependency on external AI APIs or hyperscale platforms. Clients retain full ownership of operational data.
 - Operationally scalable AI - our solution is built to scale across large asset fleets with high volume and high velocity ingestion capability and automated end-to-end ML/AI lifecycle.
 - Rapid operational deployment - integrates rapidly with existing systems. AI models developed into production in minutes once data is connected (no-code AI model development).
 - Use cases - strong cases developed over years of working in industry, including predictive asset maintenance, asset/fleet optimisation, supply chain & logistics, life-of-type extension, monitoring and support of remote facilities.
