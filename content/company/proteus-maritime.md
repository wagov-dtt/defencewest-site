## Overview

Proteus Maritime is a veteran-owned, Perth-based defence technology company delivering sovereign undersea systems for the maritime domain. Established to address critical gaps in Australia's undersea awareness, we build trusted, attritable capability designed to give the ADF an asymmetric advantage.
## Capabilities

Proteus Maritime delivers systems that provide persistent situational awareness in the maritime domain, connecting decision makers with the undersea environment in near real time. Our communications systems operate autonomously, integrate into existing battle management systems, and are deployable by small teams across a range of platforms.
## Discriminators

Veteran-owned Australian SME, 100% sovereign onshore IP
Trusted, secure systems designed and built in Australia
Attritable and scalable: priced to enable mass deployment
Autonomous operation with no platform dependency once deployed
Direct integration with existing ADF command systems
