## Overview

Norwest Connect brings together three operations that keep Australia's remote north moving: Mungalalu North Kimberley Airport (the largest all-weather airport in the far north Kimberley), Norwest Marine Operations, and Troughton Island. 
## Capabilities

Over the last 20 years, both MNKA and Troughton Island has seen increased usage from the Australian Army and Border Force. Both facilities have been used to support operations through use of the camp facility, the airfield at MNKA, to support operations in the region and Troughton Island for ISR activities in relation to Operation Sovereign Borders and Operation Resolute Deployments. Previous Users include:
Australian Army 5 RAR, 1 CSR, 1HB, 3HB, 2 EHS, 1,2,3 SQN TPR, NORFORCE, 51FNQ RFSG,10th Force Support Battalion, LCM8 refueling, RAA 8/12, 20 REG RAA, RAAF 452 SQN, 453 SQN
