## Overview

Part Engineering is a manufacturer in Osborne Park, Perth, operating under Parto Trading Pty Ltd (trading since 2015). It makes parts and components for mining, marine, oil and gas and industrial supply chains across WA, with an in-house abrasive waterjet and CNC machining, turning, wire EDM and fabrication capability.

Part Engineering has cut parts for WA companies that supply the marine and defence sectors, including duplex stainless blanks for ship work and other blanks understood to be for defence supply chains. Details of the end projects were not shared with Part Engineering, so these are listed as supply chain work rather than defence contracts. Part Engineering now offers the same fast, practical manufacturing support directly to defence primes and their suppliers in WA.
## Capabilities

Capabilities relevant to defence:
- Abrasive waterjet cutting of steel up to 200 mm on a 4,064 x 2,032 mm table, with Tilt-A-Jet taper control. Cold cutting with no heat-affected zone, suited to stainless, duplex, aluminium, composites and non-metallics.
- 5-axis CNC machining, CNC turning and wire EDM.
- Welded fabrication arranged as part of the job.
- Part remakes and reverse engineering from samples, drafting and CAD, 3D printing.
- Prototypes, one-off parts and short production runs.

Recent examples (customer names withheld):
- Duplex stainless blanks for ship work, cut for a WA company working in marine and defence.
- 120 mm glass sphere matrix foam in a fibreglass shell.
- 120 mm duplex cut with custom workholding.
- A single 8 m part in 10 mm stainless cut from 8 x 2 m plate by indexing on the table.
