## Overview

Defence Economy is a national business intelligence platform focused on the companies, people, contracts, projects, investment and opportunities shaping Australia’s defence economy. It combines journalism, data and intelligence, events and connections to help decision-makers understand and navigate the sector.
## Capabilities

Our focus is the business of defence: where investment is flowing, who is making decisions, which companies are winning and losing, what capability is being built, and what those decisions mean for Australian business. We cover military capability, policy and geopolitics where they shape investment, industrial capacity, sovereign capability and commercial opportunity.
## Discriminators

Our job is to give business leaders useful intelligence about the people, companies, projects, money, risks and opportunities shaping the defence economy. We will report the news, but our job is also to explain its commercial consequences and what decision-makers should be watching next.
