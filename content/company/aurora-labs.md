## Overview

Aurora Labs Ltd is a Perth-based advanced manufacturing company founded in 2014 and listed on the ASX in 2016. Built on a decade of metal additive manufacturing (AM) expertise, the company has focused that capability on defence and aerospace, specialising in sovereign propulsion for uncrewed and attritable platforms.

In 2024 Aurora Labs designed, built and test-fired a 200N-class micro gas turbine (MGT) with 3D-printed metal components, followed by a successful maiden flight. That year it was also contracted by Defence to advance additively manufactured propulsion technology and signed an MoU with Australian UAS developer Innovaero. In 2025 it completed the design, build and test of the AU4, a 40 kg-thrust MGT whose printed casing integrates the exhaust nozzle, engine mounts and gas seal, removing 23 parts compared with a conventionally made engine.

Aurora Labs has since moved from development into production- delivering its first AU4 propulsion systems to Australian defence company Sovereign Propulsion Systems, completing its AS9100D certification, securing Defence Industry Development Grant funding to scale manufacturing, and entering a three-year teaming agreement with MBDA. It has also supplied printed parts directly to the Department of Defence under repeat purchase orders.

Today Aurora Labs designs, manufactures, assembles and tests its engines in Western Australia, supporting the Defence Strategic Review's emphasis on sovereign industrial capability.
## Capabilities

Micro gas turbine propulsion- Aurora Labs designs, manufactures, assembles and tests sovereign MGT engines for UAVs, target drones, loitering munitions and other attritable platforms. Our AU-series engines use additively manufactured components to cut part count, weight and lead time. Recent example: in 2026 we delivered our first AU4 propulsion systems to Sovereign Propulsion Systems, following durability testing and a pilot production batch.

Metal AM bureau and engineering services- design for additive manufacturing, part consolidation, rapid prototyping and low-volume production of complex metal components. Recent example: supplying 3D-printed metal parts to the Department of Defence under repeat purchase orders.

Rapid design-to-test- in-house design, printing, post-processing and engine test capability means we can move from concept to test fire in months. Our first printed MGT went from design to working prototype in under four months.

Scalable sovereign production- Defence Industry Development Grant funding is expanding our Perth facility with additional metal printing, multi-axis CNC machining and thermal control systems, increasing our capacity to deliver propulsion systems at volume.
