## Overview

Established in 1985, Altrad is a global multi-services provider partnering with clients to build, protect, and extend the life of their assets. Operating across offshore and onshore sectors, the Group delivers integrated solutions to the marine, defence, Oil & Gas, energy, process, and mining industries.
Scaffolding is a core capability of Altrad, which is recognised as one of the largest providers of industrial services and access solutions globally. Leveraging its international network, the specialist expertise of Plettac Assco, and trusted Australian partners such as RMD Kwikform Australia, Altrad delivers fully integrated scaffolding solutions across design, engineering, supply, and installation. ## Capabilities

Altrad Services provides integrated industrial services across the energy, resources, infrastructure, marine, defence, and construction sectors. Its capabilities include scaffolding and alternative access solutions, rope access services, painting, abrasive blasting, specialist protective coatings, insulation, and industrial cleaning for both onshore and offshore environments. The company also delivers workforce training through its Registered Training Organisation (RTO), offering accredited and high-risk work licence training across a range of disciplines.

Through its engineering and construction capabilities, Altrad Services delivers civil and earthworks, underground services, concrete works, structural, mechanical and piping (SMP) construction, as well as electrical and instrumentation services. The business supports complex projects involving heavy lifts, confined space entry, working at heights, fabrication, installation, maintenance, and shutdown execution.

Altrad Services also provides rotating equipment maintenance, overhaul, OEM-certified parts supply, and specialised testing, inspection, and monitoring services through accredited subsidiaries. Backed by global expertise and local delivery capability, the company offers end-to-end solutions from design, fabrication, construction, maintenance, inspection, and asset integrity management through to decommissioning and ongoing operational support.
