## Overview

KALYU Pty Ltd is a Supply Nation certified, Indigenous-owned and operated business based in Wangara, Perth, Western Australia. Founded by Jake Milroy, a Palyku Traditional Owner, KALYU delivers quality chemicals, water treatment solutions and full-spectrum industrial consumables across Australia. Our chemicals are manufactured in ISO 9001-certified facilities using NATA-tested raw materials, and our supply chain has serviced remote and regional operations since 2020, including Fortescue, Hancock Prospecting, Atlas Iron and Veolia. KALYU's defence engagement includes registration on ICN Gateway and the Aboriginal Business Directory WA, participation in Land Forces 2026, and advisory engagement with the Office of Defence Industry Support (ODIS). We are building sovereign WA capability for Defence sustainment through chemical supply, water treatment and consumables, delivering Indigenous procurement outcomes on every purchase order.
## Capabilities

KALYU provides one accountable supply chain across: industrial and specialty chemicals; water treatment chemicals including flocculants, antiscalants, biocides and reverse-osmosis chemicals with dosing programs; AdBlue and AUS40 (ISO 22241); lubricants, oils, coolants and MRO consumables; hygiene, cleaning and safety products; and fuel biocides and additives, including grades in service with the Royal Navy and NATO navies. These capabilities are proven in remote, high-reliability environments: continuous supply to Fortescue operations since 2020, Tier-1 supply to Hancock Prospecting and Atlas Iron since 2024, and metropolitan supply with Veolia and Eptec. From our Perth warehouse we hold buffer stock and deliver Australia-wide, supporting sustainment for Defence bases, estates and platforms. Recent defence engagement: ODIS advisory services, ICN Gateway work-package notifications, Aboriginal Business Directory WA listing, and Land Forces 2026.
