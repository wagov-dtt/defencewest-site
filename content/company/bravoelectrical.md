## Overview

Bravo Electrical & HVAC is an Australian Veteran owned company with operations across New South Wales, South Australia, Western Australia, and Queensland, delivering specialised electrical, mechanical, HVAC and refrigeration engineering services nationwide.

Our experienced team supports naval platforms, commercial vessels, defence facilities, industrial operations, and marine infrastructure projects across Australia, providing end-to-end solutions built on quality, precision, and technical excellence.
Founded on military values, BRAVO’s culture is defined by discipline, accountability and high standards of service - principles that guide every project and ensure dependable results and long-term partnerships with our clients.

## Capabilities

Electrical Services – Safety, efficiency and long-term reliability. BRAVO provides marine, industrial and commercial electrical services across NSW, WA, SA and Far North QLD, delivering complex systems, preventative maintenance and urgent breakdown support with precision and reliability.
Reliable Industrial Refrigeration – Engineered refrigeration solutions for defence, marine, commercial and manufacturing sectors, including cold room installation, maintenance and performance monitoring to ensure reliable, efficient operations.
Systems Sustainment & Lifecycle Support – Supporting critical assets throughout their lifecycle, from identification and registration through to maintenance, decommissioning, disposal and compliance assurance, maximising asset performance and value.
Chilled Water Systems – Cost-effective, energy-efficient services delivered nationwide. Our OEM-trained technicians provide installation, maintenance and support across leading brands to ensure compliance, reliability and long-term performance.
HVAC Services – Custom HVAC solutions for marine, industrial and commercial environments. We design, install and maintain systems for performance and compliance, with proactive maintenance that extends equipment life, prevents breakdowns and reduces long-term costs.
Industrial Thermography – Precision thermal imaging delivered by Level 1 and Level 2 certified experts across Australia. Electrical and mechanical thermography helps identify faults early, improve safety and protect critical assets.
UPS & Data Room Cooling – Resilient power and cooling solutions for Australia’s most critical environments. BRAVO designs and supports UPS and data room cooling systems that protect infrastructure, maintain server stability and support uninterrupted operations.
