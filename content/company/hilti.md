## Overview

Hilti Australia has supported the local construction, infrastructure and energy sectors for decades through a direct customer model focused on innovation, quality and technical support. In relation to the defence industry, Hilti supports the delivery and upkeep of built assets that enable operational readiness, including bases, training facilities, maritime infrastructure, industrial facilities and support environments. Hilti’s role is to help contractors, engineers and asset owners improve how critical infrastructure is designed, installed, maintained and upgraded. This aligns with Hilti’s purpose of Making Construction Better, with a focus on improving productivity, safety and sustainability across complex project environments.
## Capabilities

Hilti Australia provides products, systems and services that benefit defence-related projects across construction, sustainment, maritime and facility upgrade works. Key capabilities include anchoring, direct fastening, passive fire protection, modular support systems, construction chemicals, measuring and layout tools, power tools, design support, technical documentation, digital workflow solutions, onsite training and tool services. These capabilities help customers reduce rework, improve installation quality, support safer execution and standardise applications across multiple sites or assets. Recent defence-relevant applications include support for shipbuilding and maritime programs, mechanical and electrical support installations, fire-rated penetrations, base infrastructure works and maintenance activities where speed, compliance and asset reliability are critical.
