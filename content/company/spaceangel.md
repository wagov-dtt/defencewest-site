## Overview

Space Angel Australia is a Perth-based developer of sovereign test, evaluation and launch infrastructure. Red Heaven Range at Australis Polar in the Nullarbor offers vast, uncongested land and over-sea corridors for testing interceptor drones, missiles, weapons systems and rockets, plus spacecraft re-entry. Our sister site, Australis Equatorial in Christmas Island, provides near-equatorial access. Enabled by the Australia–US Technology Safeguards Agreement, we support sovereign and allied capability and advanced manufacturing.
## Capabilities
