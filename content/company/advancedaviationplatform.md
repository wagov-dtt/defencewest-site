## Overview

Advanced Aviation Platform Pty Ltd (ACN 700 864 035) and its training company Advanced Drone Training Pty Ltd (ACN 700 865 229) were established in Western Australia by David Farrell, a RAAF officer since 2004, a former RAAF C-130H and C-17A captain (2007 to 2016), including a posting as an Air Mobility Group planning officer. The companies apply that operational aviation background to uncrewed aircraft safety, compliance and training. Our products are the Advanced Aviation Platform, a mission planning and compliance system built around the Australian CASA RPAS rules, and remote pilot courses covering BVLOS, night and high-altitude operations. In defence, Mr Farrell wrote the ADF remote pilot licence (DREPL) syllabus as a Reserve officer and was the aviation logistics representative with the WA Department of Health on Operation COVID Assist in 2020.
## Capabilities

Mission planning and airspace checks for uncrewed operations, with automatic document and approval tracking against the CASA rules.
Remote pilot, BVLOS, night and high-altitude training and syllabus development. Mr Farrell wrote the ADF DREPL syllabus.
Aviation planning and logistics. On Operation COVID Assist (2020) he prepared the Aeromedical Evacuation Plan and the Air Logistics and Transport Plan for WA Health.
Air mobility operations experience from C-130H and C-17A command and Air Mobility Group planning.
An uncrewed-threat awareness training program is in development (counter-small-UAS, for government and critical infrastructure).
