## Overview

Martin Engineering WA does not yet hold a formal track record in the defence sector, but our core capability set has been built in an environment with comparable demands: tight tolerances, rigorous quality assurance and high-consequence reliability, delivered under real time pressure for Tier 1 mining companies and leading equipment OEMs.

For over 40 years we have provided precision line boring (22mm to over 1m), specialist on-site and portable machining with custom jigs and laser-verified quality assurance, slew milling to 5m, and welding and fabrication (GMAW, MMAW, GTAW, CNC oxy/plasma cutting) to plant and equipment where failure is not an option. Our 3D laser scanning and metrology, accurate to 0.02mm/m, supports inspection, reverse engineering and CAD/CAM outputs comparable to defence sustainment and asset integrity requirements.

Our mobile fleet of purpose-built service vehicles and 24-hour service model mean we regularly deploy technicians and equipment to remote and operational sites across Western Australia, rather than requiring assets to come to us, a model directly applicable to fleet, vessel or base-level sustainment work.

We already serve mining, manufacturing, refining and processing, agriculture, automotive, transport and construction clients to the same exacting standard defence work demands, backed by a skilled, trade-qualified workforce and a culture that treats people as our greatest asset.

We see defence as a natural extension of the precision engineering, fabrication and mobile maintenance disciplines we already practise daily, and we are actively building the systems, accreditations and relationships needed to formalise that transition.
## Capabilities

Martin Engineering WA's core capabilities translate directly to defence sustainment needs: precision line boring (22mm to over 1m), specialist on-site and portable machining with custom jigs and laser-verified QA, slew milling to 5m, welding and fabrication (GMAW, MMAW, GTAW, CNC oxy/plasma cutting), 3D laser scanning and metrology to 0.02mm/m, and a mobile fleet of purpose-built service vehicles offering 24-hour response across Western Australia. These are the same tight tolerances, quality assurance and rapid-mobilisation standards defence work demands, proven daily for Tier 1 miners and equipment OEMs.

Recent relevant example: a defence-sector client engaged Martin Engineering to assess and quote a specialised thread repair solution on a naval vessel, after locating us through an AI-assisted (Microsoft Copilot) search. Our team scoped the damage, developed a repair method and tooling approach, and provided a quoted solution within the client's timeframe. In the end the repair proved not to be required, but the engagement demonstrated our ability to rapidly assess an unfamiliar, high-consequence asset, propose a technically sound fix, and be ready to mobilise on defence-relevant timelines.

We see this as an early signal of fit rather than a track record: our precision machining, in-situ repair and mobile response capabilities are readily transferable to naval, land vehicle and base-level sustainment work, and we are keen to build a genuine defence history from here.
