## Overview

Pixel Analytics (Earth Observation Pixel Analytics) is an independent advisory business based in Denmark, Western Australia, led by Dr Jasmine Muir. It draws on more than 25 years across environment, space missions, remote sensing and spatial science in government, research and industry. That includes helping to shape the technology strategy for Landgate’s $140M Spatial WA digital twin, conceptualising SmartSat CRC’s sustainable agriculture digital twin program, contributing to several of Australia's satellite mission planning leadership teams (including the national space mission, Aquawatch, OzFuel and Kanyini), and extensive Earth observation data quality work with Geoscience Australia. Pixel Analytics is not tied to any data provider or platform, and brings in specialists from a trusted network when a project needs more depth.
## Capabilities

Pixel Analytics helps defence and space organisations make sure Earth observation (EO) and spatial data can be trusted for the decisions it supports.

Capabilities relevant to defence:

EO data quality, calibration and validation: assessing whether satellite and airborne data are accurate, consistent and fit for purpose.
User needs and requirements: turning operational questions into clear requirements for EO products, sensors and missions.
Independent technical review: testing proposed methods, providers and technologies before investment, and identifying unnecessary complexity.
Spatial data strategy and architecture: shaping how geospatial information is governed, integrated and shared across organisations.
Environmental evidence for estates and training areas: monitoring design and field-to-satellite integration for land condition, vegetation and environmental compliance.

Recent examples:

Geoscience Australia: extensive work on the quality and fitness for purpose of national EO data, the foundation data many defence and security users rely on.
Australian Space Agency: contributed to the concurrent design study and the development of EO requirements for the National Space Mission for Earth Observation, translating user needs into mission requirements.
SmartSat CRC: developed the sustainable agriculture program and contributed to the AquaWatch senior leadership team, setting requirements and roadmaps for a national satellite mission.
Landgate, Spatial WA: shaped the strategy and architecture of the $140M Spatial WA digital twin, aligning government, industry and technical stakeholders on priorities, governance and investment.
FrontierSI: shaped complex spatial initiatives across government, industry and research, from problem definition through to proposals and delivery plans.
## Discriminators

Independent: not tied to any satellite operator, data provider, platform or software, so advice is driven by the requirement, not a product.
Problem first, technology second: we define the operational question and the evidence needed before recommending sensors, data or methods, which avoids unnecessary cost and complexity.
Mission-level EO experience: contributed to mission design and requirements for Australia’s National Space Mission for Earth Observation (NSMEO), working with requirements from a range of government users.
Depth across the full chain: 25 years spanning field measurment for environmental applications; satellite, drone and airborne remote sensing; multispectral, hyperspectral, lidar and radar; data quality and spatial strategy, from ground truth through to national programs.
Senior, flexible engagement: fixed-fee reviews, day-rate advice or fractional program support, giving clients senior judgement without a full-time hire.
Western Australian and regional base (Great Southern), working with clients across Australia and internationally, with a trusted network of specialists for larger tasks.
