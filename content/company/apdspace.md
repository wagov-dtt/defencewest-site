## Overview

APD Space is a space-focused spin-off of APD Global, a Western Australian engineering company with more than 25 years of experience in critical infrastructure engineering, telecommunications, power and automation, including delivery in remote and security-critical environments. APD Global has contributed to major projects of national importance, including Telstra’s OneWeb LEO satellite backhaul rollout and engineering support for the SKA-Low Telescope. APD Space was established as a natural extension of these capabilities, moving from engineering critical assets on the ground to developing sovereign near-space and space technologies that can monitor, inspect and protect them from above. Together, this provides a comprehensive ground-to-space approach to strengthening the resilience of Australia’s essential infrastructure and strategic assets.
## Capabilities

APD Space operates near-space stratospheric platforms (18-35 km) for customer payload hosting and technology demonstration, while also developing integrated proprietary payloads for observation, remote sensing and communication. Operating above conventional air traffic and most of the atmosphere, the platforms provide a unique high-altitude vantage point for wide-area coverage, with a line-of-sight horizon extending hundreds of kilometres. Rapid deployment, achievable within hours of mission readiness, supports responsive missions, as well as pre-orbit payload validation. In parallel, APD Space is developing its first orbital payload combining radio-frequency (RF) sensing to detect and characterise signals from the ground, optical imaging to provide visual information over the same areas, and an advanced Positioning, Navigation and Timing (PNT) technology for precise timing. Fusing RF, optical and positioning data can link detected signals with their location, timing and visual context, creating a more complete picture of activity within an area of interest. Applications include RF interference monitoring, critical infrastructure monitoring, maritime and airspace monitoring, remote-area surveillance and environmental observation.

• Multi-layered ""ground – near-space – orbit"" expertise: together with our parent company APD Global, we combine proven ground infrastructure engineering with sovereign eyes and ears in air, near-space, and soon in orbit. 
• Near-space as an operational layer: the only WA provider of wide-area sensing, observation and communications from 18-35 km, with missions deployable within hours of readiness.
• Sovereign Australian capability: platforms, payloads and flight systems engineered and operated in Western Australia, backed by the national and international presence of our parent company APD Global.
• Multi-sensor intelligence: our orbital concept combines RF detection, optical imagery and resilient timing, linking what is detected with where, when and what is happening on the ground.
• Parent company heritage: APD Global brings over 25 years of critical-infrastructure engineering, collaboration with ASD and ANSTO, security-cleared personnel, award-winning digital delivery (e.g., 2026 Bentley Year in Infrastructure Award for the design delivery on Clean Energy Link - North in Western Australia), advanced high-voltage and operational technology cybersecurity expertise, and in-house testing and simulation capability.
