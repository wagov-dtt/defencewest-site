## Overview

Chess Engineering is a turnkey solution for your defence asset, estate and vessel needs. We are a fully integrated metal fabrication through installation and project management solution with CC3 certification for both metal fabrication (ARC, MIG, TIG welding, heavy load machining) and erection/installation (with our dedicated site team, welding inspectors and project managers). You would benefit from over 50 years of experience working with blue-chip companies such as Metso, FL Smidth, BMD, CPB and more on major infrastructure and fixed plant projects throughout WA and NSW; which shows in our dedication to quality and excellence in customer experience through projects up to $40M. 

We are looking to form strategic partnerships with primes and tier 2 companies to explore vessel component fabrication, facility maintenance, installation and cradle-to-grave supply and install projects for facilities and light/heavy-duty mechanical parts across the defence sector.
