## Overview

Epsilon Systems Australia is a wholly owned subsidiary of Epsilon Systems Solutions, Inc. (Epsilon Systems), a diversified professional and technical services company based in San Diego, CA – USA. Epsilon Systems was founded in 1998 and is 100% employee owned.   Epsilon Systems Australia was established for AUKUS, to serve the men and women of the Australian, United Kingdom and United States submarine forces.
## Capabilities

In support of the fleet, Epsilon Systems provides marine engineering, assessment, maintenance and repair capabilities for all classes of U.S. Navy aircraft carriers, surface ships, submarines, and service craft.   Our Epsilon C5I subsidiary provides C5ISR and battlespace management support including software development, systems engineering, cyber security, program management, and training solutions.  Our Engineering and Advanced Products group provides design, assembly, and testing for a range of electric, electronic, and mechanical systems used in military and commercial applications.
Subject Matter Expert support for SUBSAFE, Lead Maintenance Activity requirements, Emergency Planning, and Skills Training (initial and proficiency sustainment)
Maintenance, certification, storage, and pickup/delivery of Platforms and Service Equipment in Western Australia
Construction, procurement, and manufacturing of Platforms, Service Equipment, and Mockups
