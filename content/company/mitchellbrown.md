## Overview

Established more than 30 years ago in regional Western Australia, Mitchell & Brown Communications delivers integrated communications, electronic security, fire protection and technology services to commercial, industrial, government and critical infrastructure sectors. Through long-standing relationships with major contractors and industry partners, the company has contributed to a range of projects supporting essential infrastructure across the Mid West and broader Western Australia.

The business has experience working in secure and highly regulated environments and has supported prime contractors involved in government and defence-related programs through the delivery and maintenance of communications, security and life-safety systems. This background has provided a strong foundation for the company's continued growth into emerging technology and cyber security services supporting critical industries and defence supply chains.

*** We are unable to discuss specific projects or engagements due to strict confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations ***
## Capabilities

We provide integrated communications, electronic security, fire protection and technology services across Western Australia. Our capabilities include communications infrastructure, structured cabling, wireless and radio systems, CCTV, access control, intrusion detection, fire detection and alarm systems, inspection and maintenance services, as well as emerging cyber security and managed IT capabilities.

These services are directly relevant to organisations operating within the defence sector and broader defence supply chain, where secure, reliable and compliant infrastructure is critical to operations. Our experience working in regulated environments has developed a strong understanding of safety, security, quality assurance and site access requirements.

We have supported major infrastructure, industrial and critical asset projects throughout WA, including supporting projects within secure and controlled environments where personnel were required to satisfy site-specific screening, clearance and access requirements. Due to contractual, security and confidentiality obligations, further details regarding specific facilities, projects and clients cannot be disclosed.

Building on its established communications, security and fire protection capabilities, MBC is expanding into cyber security and managed technology services. This will enable the company to support defence contractors and supply chain participants seeking to improve cyber resilience, strengthen security governance and progress towards Defence Industry Security Program (DISP) requirements. Through this approach, MBC aims to provide a trusted, locally based Western Australian partner capable of supporting physical, electronic and cyber security outcomes across the defence sector.
## Discriminators

Following the recent acquisition of Mitchell & Brown Communications (MBC), the new ownership has brought extensive experience in information technology, cyber security, telecommunications and digital transformation within highly regulated environments, including WA Gov critical infrastructure organisations. This complements our established capabilities in communications, electronic security and fire protection systems, creating a pathway to deliver more integrated technology and security solutions.

As part of our strategic growth plans, we are expanding into cyber security and IT disciplines. Leveraging the experience of its leadership team, the company is developing capabilities aligned to the security, resilience and compliance requirements of critical infrastructure, government and defence supply chains.

MBC sees significant opportunity to support defence contractors seeking to strengthen their cyber security maturity and meet DISP obligations. By combining cyber security and IT expertise with practical experience delivering communications, security and life-safety systems, MBC aims to be a trusted Western Australian partner supporting organisations across risk, compliance and infrastructure security.

We have experience working on projects within secure and controlled environments where personnel were required to satisfy site-specific screening, clearance and access requirements. 

MBC has contributed to major infrastructure projects across Western Australia, including nationally significant science and research initiatives, port infrastructure, mining operations and commercial industry. This includes supporting communications and technology infrastructure associated with the Square Kilometre Array program through organisations including CSIRO and Curtin University.

To further strengthen its governance and operational maturity, MBC is currently implementing management systems aligned with ISO 2700, ISO 9001, ISO 45001, and ISO 14001.
