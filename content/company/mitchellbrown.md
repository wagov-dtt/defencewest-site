## Overview

Mitchell & Brown Communications (MBC) is a proudly Western Australian owned and operated business with more than 30 years of experience delivering communications, electronic security, fire protection and integrated technology solutions.
Established in regional Western Australia, MBC supports commercial, industrial, government and critical infrastructure customers across metropolitan, regional and remote locations. The company provides end-to-end capability spanning the design, supply, installation, commissioning, testing, maintenance and ongoing support of critical communications, security and life-safety infrastructure.
MBC has contributed to major infrastructure projects across Western Australia, including nationally significant science and research infrastructure, resources operations, healthcare, government facilities and other complex regional projects. Its experience includes supporting major contractors and operating within secure, controlled and highly regulated environments where safety, security, quality, confidentiality and site access requirements are critical.
With its regional history and multidisciplinary technical capability, MBC is well positioned to support Defence, Defence Industry and prime contractors seeking capable Western Australian supply chain partners.

## Capabilities

Mitchell & Brown Communications provides multidisciplinary capability across communications infrastructure, electronic security, fire and life safety, networking, and integrated technology solutions,
Communications & Fibre Infrastructure
MBC provides structured data and communications cabling, fibre optic infrastructure, fibre termination and fusion splicing, OTDR testing, communications racks and cabinets, network infrastructure and associated testing and commissioning.
The company has experience delivering communications and fibre infrastructure in complex regional and remote environments, including major infrastructure projects requiring specialist mobilisation, quality assurance, inspection and test planning, and coordination with principal contractors and other trades.
Electronic Security
MBC designs, installs, commissions, integrates, maintains and supports electronic security systems including:
•	CCTV and IP video surveillance
•	Electronic access control
•	Intrusion detection and alarm systems
•	Integrated electronic security solutions
•	Security system maintenance and technical support
These capabilities support Defence and critical infrastructure applications requiring dependable physical and electronic security infrastructure.
Fire Protection & Life Safety
MBC's fire services capability encompasses fire detection and alarm systems, occupant warning systems, fire extinguishers and blankets, fire hose reels, hydrant systems, fire pumps, sprinkler systems and specialist fire suppression solutions, together with inspection, testing, commissioning and preventative maintenance services.
Networking & Wireless Communications
MBC delivers managed WiFi, wireless networking, structured network infrastructure and associated communications solutions for commercial, industrial and government environments. These capabilities can be delivered alongside fibre, electronic security and other technology infrastructure as an integrated solution.

## Discriminators

More than 30 years of regional Western Australian capability:
MBC has operated from regional Western Australia for more than three decades. This has developed practical experience in delivering and supporting critical systems outside metropolitan environments, including mobilisation, logistics, travel, site coordination and maintaining services where specialist technical resources may not be locally available.
For Defence activities across Western Australia's large geographic footprint, this regional capability represents a significant advantage.
Communications, Security, Fire and Technology under one business:
MBC's multidisciplinary capability enables customers and prime contractors to engage one supplier across communications infrastructure, fibre optics, CCTV, access control, electronic security, fire detection, fire protection, wireless networking and associated technology systems.
This provides opportunities to reduce interfaces between specialist subcontractors and adopt a more coordinated approach to interconnected technology and security infrastructure.
Experience supporting major and complex infrastructure:
MBC has contributed to significant infrastructure projects across Western Australia, including nationally significant science and research infrastructure, healthcare facilities, resources operations, government facilities and other regional projects.
Understanding of secure and regulated environments:
MBC has experience undertaking work within secure and controlled environments where personnel and contractors are required to comply with site-specific access, screening, safety and security requirements.
This experience provides practical familiarity with the discipline required when delivering infrastructure for government, critical infrastructure and other security-conscious customers.
